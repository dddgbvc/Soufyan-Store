Optional licensed car models
============================
Put a 3D model you have the rights to use here, named after the car it replaces:

  VortexGT  Sedan  SUV  Pickup  Taxi  Sports      (.prefab, .fbx, .obj or .blend)

Then run  NeonRush > LA > Build Los Angeles (everything).  The model is scaled to the
car's length, placed on the ground and used instead of the procedural body; physics,
AI, wheel colliders and damage stay the same. Without files here the procedural cars are used.
